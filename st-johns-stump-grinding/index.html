PROBLEM → SOLUTION

THE STUMP IS SMALL. THE DELAY IT CAUSES IS NOT.

In St. Johns County, a single stump can stop sod replacement, block a fence line, interfere with irrigation changes or take up the exact area needed for a pool, patio or landscape redesign.

Grinding reduces the visible stump below grade while limiting the larger disturbance created by full excavation.

  • Clear space for sod and planting beds
  • Prepare fence and pool project areas
  • Improve mowing access
  • Reduce trip hazards and visual clutter

FAQ

ST. JOHNS STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

How much does stump grinding cost in St. Johns?

Pricing depends on diameter, root flare, quantity, access, desired depth and cleanup.

Can you work in fenced HOA yards?

Often yes. We need photos and measurements of the gate, turns and access route.

Can the area be sodded afterward?

Usually, but chips may need to be removed and soil added before sod installation.

Do you remove all roots?

Grinding targets the stump and accessible flare, not every underground root.
