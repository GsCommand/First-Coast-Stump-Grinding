FAQ

ST. JOHNS COUNTY STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

Who provides stump grinding in St. Johns County?

First Coast serves St. Johns, Julington Creek, Durbin Creek, Nocatee, Ponte Vedra and nearby communities depending on route and access.

Can I get an estimate from photos?

Often. Send the stump, widest measurement, roots, gate and route, nearby obstacles, stump count, ZIP code and intended use afterward.

Can exposed roots be ground?

Accessible roots from a removed tree can often be included. Significant living-tree roots may affect health or stability and may require arborist review.

Can you work in fenced HOA yards?

Often, after gate width, turns, slopes, irrigation, pavers, landscaping and the full route are reviewed.

What happens to the chips?

Chip handling should be written into the quote. Chips may be left, spread, piled or removed; soil and grading may be separate.

Can the area be sodded afterward?

Yes, after excess chips are removed, suitable topsoil is installed and the area is graded for sod.
