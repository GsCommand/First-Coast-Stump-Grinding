FAQ

ST. JOHNS COUNTY STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

Can you work in fenced HOA yards?

Often, after the narrowest opening, turns, slopes, landscaping, irrigation and full route are reviewed.

Can grinding be done near a pool enclosure or pavers?

Often, after working clearance, debris control, plumbing, irrigation, finished surfaces and private lines are reviewed.

Can I get an estimate from photos?

Often. Send the stump, widest measurement, visible roots, gate, full route, obstacles, ZIP code and intended use afterward.

Can sod be restored afterward?

Yes, after excess chips are removed, suitable topsoil is installed and the area is graded while allowing for future settlement.
