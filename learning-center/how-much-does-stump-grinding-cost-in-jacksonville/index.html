Jacksonville Pricing Guide

How Much Does Stump Grinding Cost in Jacksonville?

A useful estimate must account for the entire stump, the access route and what you want the area to become afterward—not only the visible trunk diameter.

Updated August 5, 2026 · First Coast field guidance

Written for homeowners, property managers, landscapers and contractors throughout Jacksonville and Northeast Florida.

Quick answer: Jacksonville stump grinding cost depends on the widest root flare, total stump count, equipment access, requested grinding depth, nearby concrete or structures, surface roots and the cleanup plan. A clear photo estimate is usually more useful than an online calculator because two stumps with the same trunk diameter can require very different amounts of work.

Homeowners searching for stump grinding prices in Jacksonville often see very different numbers online. That is usually because the quoted jobs are not equivalent. One price may cover a small stump in an open front yard with chips left onsite. Another may include a wide oak root flare behind a narrow gate, deeper grinding for sod, surface-root work and removal of excess grindings.

The factors that change stump grinding cost

  • Widest root-flare measurement: Measure the widest part at ground level, not only the narrow cut face.
  • Number of stumps: Several small stumps may be priced differently from one large stump.
  • Tree type and condition: Dense, fresh wood may grind differently from an older, decayed stump.
  • Gate and access route: Hinges, turns, slopes, steps, soft soil, fences and pool enclosures can affect equipment access.
  • Grinding depth: Mulch, sod, a fence post, pavers or construction may require different scopes.
  • Surface roots: Long exposed roots outside the main stump footprint can add substantial grinding.
  • Nearby obstacles: Concrete, irrigation, pool plumbing, landscape lighting, private utility lines, rocks and buried metal increase planning and risk.
  • Wood-chip handling: Leaving chips onsite is different from separating chips, hauling material, adding soil and grading the area.

Why the root flare matters more than the cut face

A tree service may leave a stump with a relatively narrow top but a much wider base where the trunk transitions into structural roots. That lower root flare is the part the grinder must process. Measuring only across the top can make a large live oak, pine, cypress, palm or multi-trunk ornamental appear smaller than the actual grinding footprint.

For a useful Jacksonville photo quote, place a tape measure across the widest visible base at ground level. Include a second photo from farther back so the estimator can see surface roots, nearby concrete, fences, landscaping and the working area around the stump.

Front-yard access versus backyard access

An open front-yard stump is usually simpler to reach than a stump behind a fence, beside a pool enclosure or at the end of a narrow side yard. Backyard stump grinding may require review of the gate opening, hinge-to-hinge clearance, tight turns, air-conditioning units, pavers, drainage areas and changes in grade.

Jacksonville neighborhoods can vary considerably—from open lots and older established yards to tightly landscaped communities with irrigation, screened pools and narrow side access. The route from the street to the stump should be reviewed as carefully as the stump itself.

Are multiple stumps less expensive per stump?

They can be more efficient because the mobilization, travel and equipment setup occur once. This is why homeowners often benefit from identifying every stump before requesting a quote. However, a package price is not simply the first stump price multiplied by the total count. Size, spacing, roots, access and cleanup still matter for each location.

Number each stump in the photos and include one wide property view showing how far apart they are. For larger Jacksonville properties, fence routes or contractor projects, this helps establish whether the work can be completed as one efficient grinding sequence.

What should be included in a written stump-grinding quote?

  • Which stump or stumps are included
  • The measurement method used
  • Whether visible root flare and identified surface roots are included
  • The intended grinding depth or finished-use goal
  • Access assumptions and required gate clearance
  • How wood chips and debris will be handled
  • Whether hauling, topsoil, grading, seed or sod are included or excluded
  • Responsibility for irrigation, private lines and concealed obstacles
  • Payment terms and conditions that could change the price onsite

How to get a faster Jacksonville photo estimate

Send one close photo of the stump, one tape-measure photo across the widest root flare, one wide photo showing nearby obstacles, photos of the complete access route and a measurement of the narrowest gate opening. Include the Jacksonville ZIP code, total stump count and what you plan to do with the area afterward.

Clear photos reduce uncertainty and make it easier to distinguish a straightforward open-yard stump from a project involving tight access, pool equipment, fencing, surface roots or restoration work. Review the complete photo-estimate checklist before submitting the project.

Frequently asked questions

How is stump grinding priced in Jacksonville?

Pricing is usually based on the complete project scope: stump and root-flare size, quantity, access, depth, nearby obstacles, surface roots and cleanup requirements.

Is per-inch stump pricing accurate?

It can provide a starting point, but it may be misleading when the root flare is wider than the cut face or when access, roots and cleanup add significant work.

Can stump grinding be quoted from photos?

Many straightforward projects can be estimated from clear photos that show the stump, widest root flare, gate opening, complete access route and nearby obstacles.

Are multiple stumps cheaper to grind together?

Multiple-stump projects may be more efficient because travel and setup are shared, although each stump still adds grinding time, wear, chips and site-specific risk.

Does the quoted price include removing the wood chips?

Not automatically. Chip handling should be stated clearly in the written scope because leaving grindings onsite, hauling them away and restoring the area are different services.
