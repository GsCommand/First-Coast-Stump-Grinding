Pricing

How Much Does Stump Grinding Cost in Jacksonville?

The visible diameter is only one part of the quote.

Updated August 4, 2026 · First Coast field guide

Quick answer: Jacksonville pricing varies because root flare, access, depth, quantity, nearby structures and cleanup can change the work substantially. A project-specific photo estimate is more useful than a generic calculator.

Why online prices vary

Some published figures represent small open-yard stumps, while others include minimum service charges, difficult access, deeper grinding or cleanup. Comparing only the headline number can be misleading.

The main price factors

  • Widest stump and root-flare measurement
  • Number of stumps
  • Species, density and decay
  • Gate width, turns, slopes and soft ground
  • Requested depth and future use
  • Surface roots, rocks, concrete or embedded metal
  • Chip removal, soil and grading

Why multiple stumps can change the economics

Travel and setup can be shared across several stumps, but each stump still has its own size, access and root system. Grouped projects should be quoted as a complete scope.

What a good quote includes

The estimate should identify each stump, included roots, expected depth, access assumptions, chip handling, restoration and payment terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is per-inch pricing always accurate?

No. It can be a starting point, but wide root flare and restricted access may not be captured.

Can photos provide an estimate?

Often, when they include measurements and the complete access route.
