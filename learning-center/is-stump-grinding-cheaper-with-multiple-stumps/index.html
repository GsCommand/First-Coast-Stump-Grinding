Pricing

Is Stump Grinding Cheaper With Multiple Stumps?

Several stumps can share travel and setup, but they do not become identical jobs.

Updated August 4, 2026 · First Coast field guide

Quick answer: The average cost per stump may improve when several stumps are completed during one visit, but total pricing still depends on each stump’s diameter, root flare, access, depth and cleanup.

Where efficiency comes from

One mobilization, one equipment setup and one route can serve several stumps. This is why grouping all known stumps into one estimate can be more efficient than scheduling separate visits.

Why every stump still needs documentation

A small open-yard stump is not equivalent to a large root flare behind a gate. Number every stump and provide a separate measurement and access photo for each work area.

Factors that reduce the discount

  • Stumps spread across different access zones
  • Fence-panel or gate limitations
  • Large surface-root systems
  • Different grinding depths
  • Chip haul-away or restoration at each location

How to request a grouped quote

Send one overview of the property, then label each stump 1, 2, 3 and so on. Include measurements, roots and access. Ask for the total project scope rather than a vague per-stump number.

Frequently asked questions

Should small stumps be included?

Yes. Adding them while equipment is already onsite may be more efficient than a later visit.

Do all stumps need the same cleanup?

No. Chip handling and restoration can be specified separately by location.
