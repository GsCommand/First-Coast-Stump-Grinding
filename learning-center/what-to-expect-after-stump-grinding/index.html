Aftercare

What Should You Expect After Stump Grinding?

The visible stump is gone, but cleanup and restoration depend on the written scope.

Updated August 4, 2026 · First Coast field guide

Quick answer: Expect a mix of wood chips and soil, remaining underground roots and possible future settling. A finished lawn requires chip removal, suitable soil, grading and seed or sod.

Immediately after grinding

The work area may contain a mound of loose grindings. The pile can look larger than the original stump because the material has been cut and loosened.

What remains underground

Most deeper and lateral roots stay in place. They break down gradually and can cause the soil to settle over time.

Cleanup options

Depending on the quote, chips may be left, spread, piled or hauled away. Topsoil, grading and lawn restoration are separate unless specifically included.

Restoring the area

For turf, remove excess chips, add suitable soil, shape the grade and install seed or sod. Monitor the area and add soil later if a depression develops.

Construction projects

Do not assume routine stump grinding creates a construction-ready foundation, patio or pool area. Additional excavation or site preparation may be required.

Frequently asked questions

Will there be a hole?

The grinding area is usually filled with loose material unless a different cleanup and backfill scope is agreed.

Will roots disappear immediately?

No. Buried roots remain and decompose over time.
