How to Prepare Your Yard for Stump Grinding

A clear route and accurate site information reduce delays and prevent surprises.

Updated August 4, 2026 · First Coast field guide

Quick answer: Clear the access route, unlock gates, move vehicles and furniture, secure pets, identify private lines and show the operator exactly which stump and roots are included.

Clear the route

Move hoses, toys, planters, furniture, trash cans and portable decorations. Trim movable branches only when safe and remove anything that narrows the path.

Protect people and pets

Keep children, pets and visitors away from the work zone. Grinding equipment and flying debris require a controlled area.

Identify utilities and private systems

Disclose irrigation, pool plumbing, landscape lighting, septic components, invisible fencing and customer-installed electrical lines. Public markings may not identify private systems.

Mark the scope

Confirm every stump and surface root included in the quote. For multiple stumps, number them so there is no confusion.

Plan for chips and restoration

Decide whether chips will be left, spread, piled or removed and whether topsoil or grading is part of the work.

Frequently asked questions

Should sprinklers be turned off?

Avoid soaking the access route before service and identify sprinkler heads near the stump.

Do vehicles need to move?

Move vehicles away from the unloading route and debris area when requested.
