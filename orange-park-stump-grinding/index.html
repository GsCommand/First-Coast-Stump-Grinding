FAQ

ORANGE PARK QUESTIONS

Can you reach a stump behind a fence?

Sometimes. Gate width is only one factor; turns, slopes, steps, landscaping and the complete route also matter.

Do you grind surface roots?

Accessible roots from a removed tree may be included. Roots attached to a living tree require greater caution.

Can grass grow afterward?

Yes, after excess chips are removed, suitable topsoil is added and the area is graded for seed or sod.
