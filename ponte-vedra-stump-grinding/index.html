PROBLEM → SOLUTION

A LEFTOVER STUMP DOES NOT BELONG IN A FINISHED YARD.

Ponte Vedra properties often combine mature landscaping, paver areas, pool enclosures, irrigation and limited side access. A stump can disrupt mowing, block a redesign or interfere with a fence, pool, patio or planting project.

Grinding reduces the visible stump below grade while minimizing the excavation footprint around the surrounding property.

  • Clear room for sod, mulch and planting beds
  • Prepare fence, pool and patio project areas
  • Improve the look and usability of the yard
  • Reduce obstacles near walkways and gathering spaces

FAQ

PONTE VEDRA STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

How much does stump grinding cost in Ponte Vedra?

Pricing depends on stump size, root flare, access, quantity, depth and cleanup.

Can you work around pools and pavers?

Often yes. We review the route, clearances and surrounding surfaces before scheduling.

Can I landscape the area afterward?

Usually, but chips may need to be removed and soil added before planting or sod.

Do you remove every root?

Grinding targets the stump and accessible root flare, not every underground root.
