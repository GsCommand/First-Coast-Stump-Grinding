FAQ

PONTE VEDRA QUESTIONS

Can you work near a pool enclosure?

Often, after clearance, access, debris control, pool plumbing and nearby surfaces are reviewed.

Can roots beneath pavers be ground?

Not through intact hardscape. The covering surface generally must be removed before buried roots can be reached.

Can the area be landscaped afterward?

Usually, but chips may need to be removed and suitable soil added before planting or sod.
