FAQ

FIRST COAST SERVICE AREA QUESTIONS

Do you serve my neighborhood?

Service is based on route, project size and equipment access. Send the property address with photos for confirmation.

Do you charge more for distant service areas?

Route and travel requirements can affect pricing, especially for small isolated jobs. We include that in the quote.

Can several nearby jobs be grouped?

Route scheduling may make grouped neighborhood, contractor or property-management work more efficient.

What should I send for a photo estimate?

Send the stump, diameter reference, total stump count, access path and the intended future use of the area.
