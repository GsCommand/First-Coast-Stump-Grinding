FAQ

YULEE QUESTIONS

Are multiple stumps cheaper per stump?

Sometimes, because travel and setup can be shared. Size, spacing, terrain and access still affect the total scope.

Can grinding prepare a fence route?

It can clear the visible stump and accessible flare, but the depth and any remaining excavation needs should match the fence contractor’s requirements.

What happens to the chips?

Chips can be left, spread, piled or removed according to the written project scope.
