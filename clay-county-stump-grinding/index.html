FAQ

CLAY COUNTY STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

Who provides stump grinding in Clay County?

First Coast serves Orange Park, Fleming Island, Oakleaf, Middleburg, Green Cove Springs and nearby communities, depending on route, project size and access.

Can I receive an estimate from photos?

Often. Send the widest measurement, root flare, access route, gate, obstacles, stump count, ZIP code and intended use afterward.

Can exposed roots be ground?

Accessible roots from a removed tree can often be included. Significant living-tree roots require caution and may need arborist review.

Can you grind near a pool or fence?

Often, after reviewing clearance, debris protection, underground plumbing, irrigation and private lines.

What happens to the chips?

Chip handling should be written into the quote. Chips may be left, spread, piled or removed; soil and grading may be separate.
