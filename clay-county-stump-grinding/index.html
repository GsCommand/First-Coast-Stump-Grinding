WHAT OFTEN CHANGES A CLAY COUNTY PROJECT.

Fences and Pools

Measure gate openings and show working clearance, enclosure framing and nearby plumbing.

Multiple Stumps

Larger properties may benefit from grouped pricing when travel and setup can be shared.

Surface Roots

FAQ

CLAY COUNTY STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

Do you provide service in Orange Park and Fleming Island?

First Coast serves Clay County communities including Orange Park, Fleming Island, Oakleaf, Middleburg and Green Cove Springs, subject to route and access.

Can I receive an estimate from photos?

Often. Send the full stump, widest measurement, roots, gate and route, nearby obstacles, stump count, ZIP code and intended use afterward.

Can a grinder reach a fenced backyard?

Sometimes. The narrowest opening, turns, slopes, steps and fixed obstacles along the complete route must be reviewed.

Can exposed roots be included?

Accessible roots from a removed tree can often be included. Significant roots connected to a living tree may require arborist review.
