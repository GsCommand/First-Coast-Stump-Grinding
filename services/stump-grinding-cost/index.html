IMPORTANT PRICING CONTEXT

WHY ONLINE STUMP-GRINDING CALCULATORS CAN BE MISLEADING.

Many calculators multiply the visible trunk diameter by a generic per-inch number. That can be a rough starting point, but it may not reflect the actual work zone.

Two stumps with the same cut surface can have very different root flares, access routes, surface roots and cleanup requirements. A low calculator figure may also omit a minimum service visit.

  • Minimum mobilization and setup
  • Root flare beyond the flat cut
  • Gate width, turns, slopes and soft ground
  • Requested grinding depth
  • Surface roots and multiple work areas
  • Fences, pools, concrete, irrigation and private lines
  • Chip removal, imported soil and finish grading

FLORIDA-SPECIFIC CONDITIONS

SPECIES CAN MATTER, BUT SPECIES ALONE DOES NOT SET THE PRICE.

Jacksonville-area projects may involve live oak, water oak, laurel oak, pine, palm, bald cypress and crepe myrtle. Grinding difficulty varies with density, decay, root flare, embedded soil and access. Broad hardwood flares may take longer than small isolated stumps, but the actual site should control the estimate.

WRITTEN SCOPE

WHAT YOUR QUOTE SHOULD EXPLAIN

Which stump and roots are included?

Each stump should be identified, and the quote should state whether root flare or outward surface roots are included.

What grinding depth is included?

The scope should connect depth to the next use rather than relying on vague “below-grade” language.

What happens to the chips?

Confirm whether they are left, spread, piled or removed, and whether topsoil or grading is included.

What access and utility assumptions apply?

The quote should state machine access requirements and customer responsibilities for private irrigation, pool plumbing, lighting, septic components and hidden lines.

What are the payment terms?

Payment timing, deposits and any scope-change process should be clear before work begins.
