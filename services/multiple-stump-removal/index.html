FAQ

MULTIPLE STUMP QUESTIONS

Are multiple stumps cheaper per stump?

Sometimes, because travel and setup may be shared. Actual pricing still depends on each stump’s size, roots, access and location.

How should I photograph several stumps?

Number every stump and provide a separate measurement and close photo for each, plus wide photos showing access and distance between work areas.

Can surface roots be included?

Yes, when identified in the quote. Photograph and label the roots associated with each removed tree.

Can stumps in different parts of the property be handled together?

Often, but each route, gate, slope and obstacle must be reviewed before the project is scheduled.
