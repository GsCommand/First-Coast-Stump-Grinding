ACCESS MATTERS

A STUMP GRINDER HAS TO REACH THE WORK AREA SAFELY.

Backyard stumps are often located behind privacy fences, pool enclosures, sheds, landscape beds or narrow side yards. The equipment route may include tight turns, soft ground, pavers, slopes and irrigation components.

That is why a useful estimate includes access photos and measurements—not just a close-up of the stump.

  • Measure the narrowest gate or opening
  • Photograph turns and side-yard restrictions
  • Identify pools, pavers, irrigation and drainage
  • Show the stump in relation to fences and structures

FAQ

BACKYARD STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

How wide does the gate need to be?

Required width depends on the equipment selected for the project. Measure the narrowest opening and send a photo so access can be evaluated accurately.

Can equipment travel over pavers or near a pool?

That depends on the route, equipment weight, surface condition and available protection. Finished surfaces and pool areas should be shown clearly during the estimate.

Can you work close to a fence?

Often yes, but clearance, roots, buried utilities and the fence condition must be considered.

Do you need photos of the whole yard?

You do not need to photograph everything, but the complete route from the street or driveway to the stump should be visible.
