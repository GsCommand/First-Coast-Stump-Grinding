FAQ

BACKYARD ACCESS QUESTIONS

Can a stump grinder fit through a residential gate?

Sometimes. Measure the narrowest clear point and show hinges, turns, slopes, steps and fixed obstacles along the full route.

Can you grind near a fence or pool?

Often, after working clearance, debris control, pool plumbing, irrigation and nearby hardscape are reviewed.

Will the machine damage the lawn?

Ground conditions, machine type and route affect risk. Soft soil, slopes and saturated areas may require different planning or scheduling.

Can surface roots be included?

Accessible roots from a removed tree can often be included. Living-tree roots require more caution.
