FAQ

STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

Can you estimate the work from photos?

Often. Send the stump, widest measurement, roots, gate and route, obstacles, stump count, ZIP code and intended use afterward.

Does grinding remove every root?

No. Grinding targets the stump and accessible root flare, while deeper and lateral roots remain underground.

How deep will the stump be ground?

Depth depends on the future use and site conditions. Grass, landscaping, fencing, hardscape and construction can require different scopes.

What happens to the chips?

Confirm whether chips are left, spread, piled or removed, and whether topsoil or grading is included.
