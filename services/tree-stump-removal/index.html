FAQ

STUMP REMOVAL QUESTIONS

When is full removal more appropriate than grinding?

When foundations, utilities, excavation, pools or major construction require the root crown to be removed rather than left underground.

Does full removal disturb more of the yard?

Usually. Excavation creates a larger work area and often requires backfill, compaction and restoration.

Can the scope be estimated from photos?

Photos can help establish the likely work, but construction and excavation conditions may still require onsite verification.
