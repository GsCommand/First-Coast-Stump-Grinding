FAQ

NASSAU COUNTY STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

Who provides stump grinding in Nassau County?

First Coast serves Yulee, Fernandina Beach, Callahan, Wildlight and nearby areas depending on route, project size and access.

Can I get an estimate from photos?

Often. Send the widest measurement, complete stump, surface roots, access route, gate, obstacles, number of stumps and ZIP code.

Can surface roots be included?

Accessible roots from a removed tree can often be part of the scope. Living-tree structural roots require more caution.

Can the grinder fit through my gate?

Sometimes. Measure the narrowest opening and show turns, slopes, steps and fixed obstacles along the entire route.

Can grass grow after grinding?

Yes, but excess chips generally need to be removed and replaced with suitable topsoil before seed or sod is installed.
