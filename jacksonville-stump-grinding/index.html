PROBLEM → SOLUTION

AN OLD STUMP CAN BLOCK THE BEST PART OF THE YARD.

Jacksonville properties often have mature trees, established landscaping, narrow side yards and outdoor projects competing for limited space. A leftover stump can interfere with mowing, hold moisture, attract insects or stop a fence, patio, pool or sod project before it starts.

Stump grinding reduces the visible stump below grade while avoiding the larger excavation footprint of digging out the entire root system.

  • Clear space for sod, mulch and planting beds
  • Prepare fence lines and construction areas
  • Improve mowing access and yard appearance
  • Remove trip hazards near walks and play areas

FAQ

JACKSONVILLE STUMP GRINDING QUESTIONS

How much does stump grinding cost in Jacksonville?

Pricing depends on stump diameter, root flare, quantity, access, depth and cleanup. Photos are the fastest way to establish scope.

Can you reach a stump behind a fence?

Many backyard stumps are reachable. We review gate width, turns, slopes and nearby obstacles before scheduling.

Do you remove every underground root?

Grinding targets the stump and accessible root flare. It does not excavate every underground root across the property.

What happens to the chips?

Chips may be left in the grinding area, spread for mulch or handled as an added cleanup option.
